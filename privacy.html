Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data PINO ("the App") accesses, why, and how it is used. The App is operated by an individual developer for personal content-automation purposes.

1. Data We Access

DataSourcePurpose
Basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar)TikTok Login KitIdentify which account is authorized to publish
Video publishing permissionTikTok Content Posting APIUpload and publish videos to the authorized account only

2. Data We Do Not Access

The App does not request or store your TikTok password, does not read your private messages, does not access other users' content, and does not collect data about accounts other than the one that completed authorization.

3. How Data Is Stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the developer's own device, used solely to make authorized API calls to TikTok on behalf of the account owner. Tokens are not shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party.

4. Data Retention

Tokens are retained only for as long as needed to operate the App and are deleted if TikTok access is revoked by the account owner or if the App is decommissioned.

5. Third-Party Services

To generate content prior to publishing, the App may use third-party services for stock imagery and text-to-speech narration. These services do not receive TikTok account credentials or tokens — only content generation requests unrelated to your TikTok identity.

6. Your Rights

  • You may revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time via your TikTok account settings.
  • You may request deletion of any locally stored tokens by contacting the developer.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the developer via the contact information provided in the TikTok Developer Portal application.
