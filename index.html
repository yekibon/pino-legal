AI-assisted short-form video

Create with AI. Publish with intent.

PINO is an automated content-production tool that helps an account owner create short videos — including a script, narration, visuals, and captions — and publish them to their own TikTok account.

✓

The owner stays in control

Every individual video must be reviewed and explicitly approved by the TikTok account owner inside PINO before it can be published.

What PINO does

A clear path from idea to approved post

AI assists with production while the account owner makes the final publishing decision.

  1. Generate

    PINO creates a draft short-form video using AI-assisted scripting, narration, visuals, and captions.

  2. Review and approve

    The account owner reviews that specific video inside the application and chooses whether to approve it.

  3. Publish

    Only after approval, PINO uses TikTok's official Content Posting API to publish to the owner's authorized account.

Human approval

No unreviewed automatic posting

Connecting a TikTok account does not give PINO permission to publish every generated draft. Approval is requested per video. If the owner does not explicitly approve a video, PINO does not publish it. The owner may also revoke the application's TikTok access through their TikTok account settings.
